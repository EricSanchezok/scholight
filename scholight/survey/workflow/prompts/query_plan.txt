- Use context only to recover run_dir and artifact paths.
- Read full inputs from run_dir artifacts.

Workflow:

1. Read schema/query_plan.md.
2. Extract run_dir and SurveySpec from context or from run_dir/00_survey_spec.md.
3. Produce a typed query program with core_method, mechanism, problem, benchmark, survey, citation_seed, boundary, frontier, and cross_domain queries where appropriate.
4. Write run_dir/01_query_plan.md.
5. Final response: a handoff per schema/handoff.md — run_dir, artifact path, status, counts (queries by type), and up to 5 representative query IDs under ids.

Use focused English natural-language queries because Scholight ranks precise
research questions most reliably. Preserve enough diversity for downstream
scouts to discover adjacent methods, evidence, and boundary cases.
